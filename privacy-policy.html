Privacy Policy

PeakStone Developer Account Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PeakStone LLC ("PeakStone," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information across all mobile applications, games, and digital software published under the PeakStone Developer Account on Google Play, Apple App Store, and other application distribution platforms (collectively, our "Apps" or "Services").

Our Privacy Philosophy

At PeakStone, our philosophy centers on data minimization and transparent user trust. We design our applications to operate smoothly without harvesting unnecessary personal information.

Information Collection & Usage

Our applications are primarily designed to function without requiring account creation or personal identification:

  • No Mandatory Accounts: Our offline games, arcade experiences, and utilities do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal contact details.
  • No Collection of Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical mailing addresses through our general app experiences.
  • No Sensitive Hardware Access: We do not access sensitive device sensors or data (such as contacts, camera, microphone, or photo libraries) unless an app specifically requires it for a core feature and you have provided explicit runtime permission.
  • No Precise Geolocation: We do not track or record your precise GPS location.

Diagnostics, Performance & Analytics

To ensure high stability, monitor frame rates, and quickly diagnose crashes across diverse hardware and operating systems, certain apps may process aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic logs via platform APIs (such as Google Play Console diagnostics or Apple App Store crash reports). This technical telemetry includes:

  • Device manufacturer, model, and OS version
  • System language and general performance benchmarks
  • Crash stack traces and operational error logs

This diagnostic information is aggregated, completely anonymized, and never connected to personal user profiles.

Device Permissions

When an app requires a device permission (such as local file storage to save high scores and game progress, or network access to check for app updates), the permission is used solely to deliver the immediate functionality requested by the user. Permissions can be granted, reviewed, or revoked at any time through your device settings.

Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Certain free-to-play apps or games may display non-intrusive advertisements or utilize trusted developer tools provided by industry-standard partners (such as Google AdMob or Unity). These third-party services may process anonymized device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or IDFA) in accordance with their independent privacy policies to detect invalid traffic or serve appropriate ads.

PeakStone does not sell, trade, or rent personal user data. We encourage you to review the privacy documentation of third-party platform partners:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Compliance)

We take children's online privacy very seriously. Our general-audience and family-friendly apps are built in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and platform family policies. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 where applicable by local law). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete such records.

Data Security & Local Storage

Because our applications operate client-side on your device, your local settings, progress, high scores, and game data remain stored locally on your device. We employ robust, industry-standard security protocols for any web or online communication.

Policy Updates

We may update this Developer Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new apps, updated platform standards, or legal developments. Any revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated Effective Date.
